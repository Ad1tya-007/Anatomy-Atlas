Open3DModel skeleton

"Open3DModel - Skeleton" by the Open3D project, George J.R. Maat, LUMC, Eungyeol Lee, LUMC, and contributors.
License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike (CC BY-SA).
Source: https://anatomytool.org/open3dmodel

The Open3DModel is based on the predecessor projects BodyParts3D and Z-Anatomy.
This application does not modify the glTF file. The viewer mirrors right-sided bones to show the left side.

The web application code is separate from the model. If you redistribute the model file, keep this credit and the CC BY-SA license.
